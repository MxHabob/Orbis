# Orbis v1.0.0

## Summary

Orbis 1.0.0 is the first release of an on-premise education management ecosystem for universities, school networks, vocational institutes, and academies. It brings core academic, student, examination, attendance, scheduling, and finance workflows together, with support for institutions operating one or multiple campuses.

## Added

- **Organization and identity:** Model headquarters, campuses, faculties, and departments; manage students, staff, and guardians with separate person and account records; apply inherited policies across branches.
- **Academic operations:** Configure academic years and periods, support cohort-based and credit-hour structures, and manage student progression with staged transitions and rollback support.
- **Examinations and assessment:** Coordinate exam cycles, halls, and proctors; generate seating numbers and protected secret numbers; manage assessment workflows.
- **Attendance and timetabling:** Record lesson-level and daily attendance, review medical absence excuses, and identify teacher or room conflicts when scheduling.
- **Finance:** Maintain a double-entry chart of accounts, branch sub-treasuries, and point-of-sale registers; configure tuition, fees, discounts, installments, and multi-currency receipts.
- **Documents and operations:** Create certificates, transcripts, reports, and ID cards with DocForge; store documents locally or on private infrastructure; support role-based access, audit logs, and background routines.
- **On-premise deployment:** Run the platform on institution-managed infrastructure, including PostgreSQL-backed deployments and private networks, without requiring a third-party cloud for core institutional operations.
- **Multi-application foundation:** Includes a native desktop client, API services, mobile application, CLI, scheduling solver service, and shared packages for the Orbis ecosystem.
