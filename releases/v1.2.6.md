# Orbis v1.2.6

## Summary
Orbis v1.2.6 refines the web timetable, grid, tab bar, and student/user detail views, updates academic period and subject forms, removes monthly exam periods, and adds assessment service, settings entity, and organization routes.

## Added

- remove monthly exam periods and update period/subject forms (api,web,schemas)
- add assessment service, settings entity, and organization routes
- refine timetable, grid, tabs, and student/user views (web,ui,desktop)
